Detailed Description

The gap names give the correct gap width when the ADC clock is 80 MHz.

At other clock frequencies the times will scale. The debounce time will scale by the same amount, so the settings in cycles are still valid. To find the actual ADC clock period use xsp3_get_clock_period()
